Air Travel

Assembly members support a solution to air travel emissions that allows people to continue to fly. Protecting people’s freedoms, happiness as well as benefiting business and the economy

  • ✈️ Tax frequent flyers and long-haul flights
  • ✈️ Even out the costs of air travel compared to alternatives
  • ✈️ Scrap incentives that make people fly more (e.g. air miles and first class)
  • ✈️ Engage population in making necessary changes
  • ✈️ Promote & incentivise UK holidays
  • ✈️ Speed up technical progress
  • ✈️ Influence the rest of the world
  • ✈️ Stay competitive and protect the economy
