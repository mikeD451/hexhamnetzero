Home heating and hot water

  • 🏡 A ban on new gas boilers from 2030 or 2035
  • 🏡 Simpler consumer protection measures
  • 🏡 Enforcing district heating networks
  • 🏡 Local plans for zero carbon homes
  • 🏡 Information and support funded by government
  • 🏡 Government help for everyone, or at least for poorer households
  • 🏡 Support for smaller organisations to offer energy services
  • 🏡 More tailored solutions
  • 🏡 Increased choice, including thorough steps to increase competition
  • 🏡 Reliable and clear information
  • 🏡 Solutions must work for all income groups & housing types
